Why I'm so interested in this new team, in less than a minute.

Moving like a startup. One-week loops, enabled by AI at every step.

In 2011, moving this fast took a lot of people and a lot of coffee. With the right tools, a small group can have a big impact every single week. This is the same flow we ran for years at the agency. Now, with AI, the timelines are dramatically shorter.

Aren't you happy where you are?

Very. The NetApp site is in good shape and the team is great. That's sort of the point: it runs well without me reinventing it every week. I want to be on a team where I can have a big impact and change things alongside people who want to work hard and fast.

Wait, aren't you a designer?

I started there, and it's still my unfair advantage. But at Mocavo the job was the whole funnel: positioning, search, paid, analytics, personalization and the site. Design just means the stuff I ship looks good too. And at NetApp the work goes well past design: growing organic traffic and engagement, and making sure we show up in the AI tools buyers now use to do their research.

Can one person really cover all of that?

Not forever. Early on, one person who can do most of it beats five people who each do a slice. I'd add people as the pipeline earns them, and lean on AI for the repeatable work in the meantime.

What's the catch?

I want to have numbers that I own, a seat near sales and product, and permission to try new tools without forming a committee first. That's about it.

Small team. Big swing. Let's go.
